# Support

- Documentation: `docs/`
- Quick start: `docs/guides/quickstart.md`
- Diagnostics: `docs/reference/diagnostics.md`
- Security issues: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue.
- Bug reports: use the issue template and include `fuzzfw doctor --json`,
  `summary.json` (sanitized), and the exact command.
- Commercial support/support terms: the project does not provide warranty or
  indemnity under Apache-2.0. A company distribution must define its own support
  terms.

Do not post customer firmware, crash inputs, keys, or proprietary logs publicly.
